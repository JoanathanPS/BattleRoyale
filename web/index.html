DropIsland overview

Last one on the island wins.

A top-down battle royale against 5, 12 or 20 bots. Loot weapons, armor and medkits while the safe zone closes in. It runs in your browser.

Drop in Download for desktop
Opponents
5, 12 or 20 bots, by difficulty
Safe zone
Shrinks 4 times
Outside it
5 HP lost every second

Play

Runs in the browser with a keyboard and mouse. Pick a difficulty in the game's menu, then press Play. Prefer a window of its own? Open the game in a new tab.

An AW3 hit does 135 damage, more than the 100 health you start with. You wait 5 seconds for the next shot.

Armor and healing
